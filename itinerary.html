Hanging on Hall Hill

For all you early birds out there with a zest for life and a thirst for adventure, let’s hang out together on Thursday up on Hall Hill (where Ian grew up). We’ll be doing all of the fun things one does up on the hill. This includes walking around in the woods, throwing sticks for the dog, driving the 4-wheeler, lighting a (small) fire, and having a lot of big laughs and good conversations.

We’ll have some delicious food around, plus a few drinks for those inclined to do some day drinking. If you think you can pull it off – please come hang out on Thursday! Lee (Ian’s Mom) lives on Route 19 between Jane Lew and Weston.

Get ready…

On Friday, anything could happen… and by anything I mean getting ready for the wedding!

We’re going to be hanging out over at Mary Conrad Park, strategically positioning hay bales, eating snacks, skipping stones, and taking part in many a variety of casual hijinks.

If you’d like to hang at the resort during the day you can swim in the pool, rent bikes, dip in the lake, go fishing, head over to the shooting range, and all kinds of other outdoorsy things.

Later on in the evening we’ll all head over to the Stonewall Resort to hang at the lodge, drink martinis, swig moonshine (?), and hang out by the fire pit to roast some s’mores. If you’re in the mood for an after party we can also hang out over at the cottages where some fine people will be staying for a little late night hang sesh.

Saturday

Saturday, September 12, 2015
1:30 PM – 10:00 PM

The Big Day!

Okay, here’s the rundown. We’re getting married. No, seriously! It’s true!

Starting at around 12pm or so a shuttle van will begin picking people up from Stonewall Resort and taking them over to Mary Conrad Park (where the wedding will be taking place). You can also drive over, but because there isn’t a ton of parking you may have to get creative with where you abandon your car. Even if you’re not staying at Stonewall Resort, you can still park over there and take the shuttle over. Easy peasy!

Here’s a link to Mary Conrad Park on Google Maps. Here’s a link to directions from the resort to the park. It’s pretty easy to get to. All you have to do is exit the resort, take a left, drive 1.9 miles, and then turn right into Mary Conrad Park! It’s just past the gun range…

When you arrive at Mary Conrad Park there’ll be apps (short for appetizers) and drinks for you to enjoy. There’ll also be some banjo music, a lake to gaze longingly at or skip stones in, an inflatable kayak to gaze longingly at or take a ride in, and all kinds of fun things to keep you entertained like corn hole, horseshoes, lawn darts, and whatever other outdoorsy games they sell at Wal-Mart.

The ceremony will begin at around 3:30pm. It will be short and sweet, so make sure you don’t mistime your bathroom break and miss the whole thing! After the ceremony, at around 3:45pm or so, we’ll be taking a group picture. That’s right, even if you hate having your photo taken and you normally pretend not to notice everyone else posing for a group picture, too bad, so sad… get in there and say cheese!

After the main event there’ll be a big BBQ dinner and drinks and dancing (with music provided by hometown favorite DJ Dick). The menu is shaping up to include chicken, pulled pork, mashed potatoes, mac ’n cheese, cole slaw and….. green beans? After the sun goes down and you’re ready to roll back to the resort, the shuttle van will be standing by.

After the wedding, anyone with gas left in the tank can head back to the Stonewall Resort to hang loose at the Lodge or at the cottages (where some wedding guests will be staying). Joe and JR will be in charge of the PARTY HOUSE, Y’ALL!
